原文标题：ETSI’s new committee starts building next generation of AI-native networks
元信息：战略 · 2026/10/13 · 编号 #69 · 900 m；The ETSI Technical Committee on Federated Network,
要点：
- The ETSI Technical Committee on Federated Network, Edge, Cloud and AI Technologies (TC NET) held its kick-off meeting from 7 to 9 October 2026 at ETSI headquarters in Sophia Antipolis, France
- Launched in June 2026, TC NET develops globally applicable standards for a digital infrastructure that integrates computing and AI resources with connectivity technologies, resulting in a highly programmable, intelligent and federated cloud-network-edge-device continuum
- TC NET agreed on its decision-making processes and discussed its structure, its relationship with other relevant stakeholders and open source communities, including ETSI’s Software Development Groups
- Lucía Cabanillas Rodríguez (Telefónica), Yu Zeng (China Telecom), Yuya Kuno (DOCOMO Communications Laboratories) and Walter Featherstone (Apple) were elected Vice-Chairs
- The committee’s second meeting will take place on 9-11 December 2026 at ETSI headquarters
- With over 900 member organisations from more than 60 countries across five continents, ETSI offers an open and inclusive environment for members representing industry, research institutions, academia, governments, and public bodies

背景补充：据 Critical Communications Review（2026/05/14）二手报道：ETSI 此前于 2026/05/12–13 启动姊妹机构 TC CCS，承接3GPP 与 GSMA 既有工作，对应 EUCCS 系统计划 2030 年建成——TC NET 与 TC CCS 同属 ETSI 在 2026 年密集重组的关键通信与 AI 网络基础设施治理路径。
